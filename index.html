CHAPTER I — THE UNKNOWN DESTINATION

THE MAZAUA CONTROVERSY

Limasawa or Masao, Butuan?

Every voyage begins with a question. Every discovery brings us closer to the truth.

In March 1521, the fleet of Ferdinand Magellan anchored at an island its chronicler called Mazaua. There, on Easter Sunday, March 31, a Mass was celebrated. Five centuries later, historians are still asked: where, exactly, was Mazaua?

Two answers have competed for generations: Limasawa, off the southern tip of Leyte, and Masao, near the mouth of the Agusan River at Butuan. You are about to sail with the evidence and judge for yourself. No verdict yet — first, the voyage.
